# Let people choose a pickup time

Saturday mornings are chaos: everyone shows up at 9. When people order, I want them to choose a half-hour pickup time while we're open. We can handle 4 orders in each half hour.

We need at least 2 hours to get an order ready, and birthday cakes and catering trays need 2 days because we bake them to order.

On the orders page, show me the pickup times and list the orders in the order I'll be handing them out.
