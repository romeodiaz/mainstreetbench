# Cancelling an order

Use your private confirmation link to cancel unpaid bread and pastry orders before the day of pickup. Birthday cakes and catering need at least 48 hours’ notice. For same-day, paid orders, or shorter notice, call 555-010-0000 so staff can review the order and any refund.

Cancellation restores credit used from a gift card and voids unused cards bought on the cancelled order. A purchased card that has already been spent requires staff review. Recording a cancellation does not issue a cash or card refund; staff must handle and record that separately.
