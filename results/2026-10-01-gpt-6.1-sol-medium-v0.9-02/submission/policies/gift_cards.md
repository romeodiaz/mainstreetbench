# Gift card terms

Gift cards can be used in the bakery and online. They have no expiry date and no service or inactivity fees.
Cards bought online become spendable once staff record payment at pickup.
Unused, paid gift cards can be refunded within 14 days with proof of purchase, consistent with our refund policy. Once partly used, please contact the bakery for review. Cards cannot be exchanged for cash except where required by law.
Please contact us about lost or stolen cards with proof of purchase; replacement depends on verifying the card and its remaining balance.
