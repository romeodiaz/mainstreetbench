# Gift card terms

Gift cards can be used in the bakery and online. Gift cards don't expire for at least 5 years after purchase.
There are no service, dormancy or other fees on gift cards.
An unused gift card can be refunded within 14 days of purchase (see our refund policy). After that, gift cards are non-refundable and can't be exchanged for cash.
Lost or stolen cards can't be replaced.
