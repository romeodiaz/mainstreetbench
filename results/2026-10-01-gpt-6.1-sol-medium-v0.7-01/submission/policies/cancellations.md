# Cancelling an order

Use your private confirmation link to cancel an unpaid order before its pickup day, or call 555-010-0000. On the pickup day, or if payment has already been recorded, call us. Gift cards that have already been used require staff review. Cancelling releases the time and item capacity and restores any gift-card value used. Purchased gift cards are voided. Staff must separately arrange any refund of money already paid.
