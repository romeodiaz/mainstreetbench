# Gift card terms

Gift cards can be used in the bakery and online. Gift cards expire 1 year after purchase.
Unused gift cards can be refunded within 14 days of purchase, as stated in our refund policy. Otherwise they can't be exchanged for cash, except where required by law.
Lost or stolen cards can't be replaced.

Online gift cards become usable only after staff confirms payment at pickup. Placing an order alone does not activate the credit.
