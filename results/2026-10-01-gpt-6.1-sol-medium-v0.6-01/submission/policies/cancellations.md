# Cancelling an order

Use your private confirmation link to cancel at least 48 hours before your pickup time, or call 555-010-0000. For later requests, please call the bakery; staff must review them. An order that bought a gift card already used cannot be cancelled automatically. Cancellation restores any gift card balance spent and voids gift cards bought in that order. Orders are paid at pickup; any cash/card refund must be handled by staff separately.
