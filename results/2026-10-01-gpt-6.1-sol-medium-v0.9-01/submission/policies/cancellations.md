# Cancelling an order

Use your private confirmation link to cancel an unpaid order at least 48 hours before its pickup time, or call the bakery at 555-010-0000. Closer to pickup, call us so staff can check production before agreeing to cancellation.
If you have already paid, or a gift card bought with the order has been used, call us to arrange cancellation and any refund. Gift card credit used on a cancelled order is restored; gift cards purchased by that order are voided.
