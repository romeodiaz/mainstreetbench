# Gift card terms

Gift cards can be used in the bakery and online after payment is received. They do not expire and have no service or inactivity fees.
Unused gift cards can be refunded within 14 days of purchase, with proof of purchase. Contact the bakery about any cash redemption required by applicable law.
If a card is lost or stolen, contact us with proof of purchase; we will investigate whether an unused balance can be protected and replaced.
Cancelling an unpaid order voids any gift cards it bought. Gift card amounts used on a cancelled order are restored once. If a purchased card has already been used, call us to arrange cancellation.
