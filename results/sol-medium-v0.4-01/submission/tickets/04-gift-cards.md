# Let people spend gift cards online

People buy gift cards on the site, but there's no way to spend them online. They have to bring the card in.

When someone buys a gift card, give them a gift card code on their order page. At checkout, let people enter a code to pay with it. If the card has more on it than the order, keep the rest for next time. Show them how much came off the card and what's left.
